# Game rules

## Deck and setup

There are 108 cards. Red (◆), Yellow (✦), Green (●) and Blue (▲) each have one 0, two of each number from 1 through 9, two Skip, two Reverse and two Draw Two cards. Four Wild and four Wild Draw Four cards have no printed color.

Shuffle, deal seven cards to each of two to four players, then reveal the first number in the remaining deck. Any action or Wild cards ahead of that number stay in the draw pile in order. The first seat starts clockwise.

## On your turn

Play a card matching the active color, number or action type. Either Wild is always legal on a ready turn. Wild Draw Four has no stacking or challenge. Choose the new color before the Wild enters the discard pile and play continues.

You may draw one card even if you have a match in hand. If the draw is legal, you may play that specific card immediately or end your turn. An unplayable draw ends the turn automatically.

| Action   | Result                                                                                            |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Skip     | Pass over the next seat in the current direction.                                                 |
| Reverse  | Flip direction and advance in the new direction. With two players, also skip the other player.    |
| Draw Two | The next player draws up to two available cards and is skipped. The penalty resolves immediately. |
| Wild     | Choose any of the four active colors.                                                             |

Normal plays advance one seat. Skip and Draw Two advance two seats. A two-player Skip, Reverse or Draw Two gives the current player a fresh turn, with the same board and hand visible. Draw Two penalties have no response window, so they cannot be stacked.

When the draw pile runs out, shuffle older discards into a new draw pile and leave the current discard visible. If there are no recyclable cards, a voluntary draw ends the turn. A Draw Two penalty still skips its target when fewer than two cards are available.

The first player to empty their hand wins. Any last-card effect resolves first, including a Wild's color choice and a Draw Two penalty. The game ends before another turn starts. Illegal commands leave the state unchanged. A rematch reshuffles, deals again, and starts with the first seat.

**Color Clash house rule.** Choose **End & Score** from the game menu to finish early. The player with the fewest cards wins, and every player sharing the lowest count ties. Scoring uses the cards currently in hand, including a pending Wild whose color has yet to be chosen. **Exit Game** instead clears the local match without a result.

Wild Draw Four sets the chosen color, gives the next player up to four available cards, and skips that seat. The draw-pile recycling and partial-penalty rules apply.
