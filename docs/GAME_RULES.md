# Game rules

## Deck and setup

There are 108 cards. Red (◆), Yellow (✦), Green (●) and Blue (▲) each have one 0, two of each number from 1 through 9, two Skip, two Reverse and two Draw Two cards. Four Wild and four Wild Draw Four cards have no printed color.

Shuffle, deal seven cards to each of two to four players, then reveal the first number in the remaining deck. Any action or Wild cards ahead of that number stay in the draw pile in order. The first seat starts clockwise.

## On your turn

Play a card matching the active color, number or action type. Either Wild can be played on a regular turn. Choose the new color before the Wild enters the discard pile and play continues. There is no Wild Draw Four challenge.

You may draw one card even if you have a match in hand. If the draw is legal, you may play that specific card immediately or end your turn. An unplayable draw ends the turn automatically.

| Action         | Result                                                                                         |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Skip           | Pass over the next seat in the current direction.                                              |
| Reverse        | Flip direction and advance in the new direction. With two players, also skip the other player. |
| Draw Two       | Pass a +2 penalty to the next seat.                                                            |
| Wild           | Choose any of the four active colors.                                                          |
| Wild Draw Four | Choose the active color and pass a +4 penalty to the next seat.                                |

Normal plays advance one seat. Skip advances two seats. With two players, a Skip or Reverse gives the current player a fresh turn.

## House rule: identical number cards

This option starts off. Enable it during bot setup or as the host before starting an online match. On a regular turn, you can play a group of number cards with the same color and value if the first card is legal. Select cards in the order you want to play them. The group uses one turn, and the last selected card becomes the top discard. Each card counts as a play in the match summary. After an ordinary draw, only the drawn card can be played.

## House rule: penalty stacking

The next player can stack a Draw Two on a Draw Two of any color, or a Wild Draw Four on a Wild Draw Four. A Wild Draw Four stack requires a color choice before the penalty advances. Regular plays, batches, and ordinary draws are unavailable while a penalty is pending.

The target may accept the full penalty even when they can stack. They draw as many cards as are available and lose their turn. Play then advances to the next seat in the current direction. In a two-player game, stacking passes the penalty back to the other player, and accepting returns play to the other player. A chain follows the direction in effect when it starts. Skip and Reverse cannot interrupt a chain.

When the draw pile runs out, shuffle older discards into a new draw pile and leave the current discard visible. If there are no recyclable cards, a voluntary draw ends the turn. A penalty is cleared once accepted, even when fewer cards than requested are available.

## Ending the match

The first player to empty their hand wins, including with an identical-number batch. Last-card effects resolve before the win. If a last card starts or extends a draw penalty, the next player draws the full accumulated amount where available, with no further stacking. A last Wild still requires a color choice. Illegal commands leave the state unchanged. A rematch keeps the configured rules and deals a fresh deck.

Choose **End & Score** to finish early. The player with the fewest cards wins, and every player sharing the lowest count ties. It uses the current hands, including a Wild awaiting its color choice. Pending penalties end without a draw. **Exit Game** leaves without recording a result.
